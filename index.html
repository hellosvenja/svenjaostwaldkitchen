What’s in a prep day

The cooking is only part of it. A prep day is six to seven hours of focused work, which puts my fee at roughly $100 an hour, for a bestselling cookbook author and certified health coach who plans, shops and cooks for you.

  1. ~1 hMenu design and nutrition planningA personal menu built around your goals, allergies and the people at your table.
  2. ~1 hSourcing the groceriesI shop myself, so every ingredient meets my standard: organic where possible, no added sugar, short ingredient lists.
  3. 2–4 hCooking in your kitchenFour dishes, prepared fresh where you live.
  4. ~30 minPacking, labeling, cleanupEverything portioned and labeled, your kitchen left spotless.
  5. + travelTo and from your homeAnywhere in Manhattan and Brooklyn, no extra fee.

Good to know

Do you cater to allergies and dietary preferences?
Yes. Every menu is planned around you, whether you’re vegetarian, gluten-free or simply have a list of things you don’t like.
How long does a prep session take?
Between 2 and 4 hours, depending on your menu.
Which areas do you serve?
Manhattan and Brooklyn.
Can I book weekly?
Yes. Weekly, every two weeks or just once: we’ll find the rhythm that fits your life.
Why do you do the grocery shopping yourself?
Because the ingredients are half the result. I choose every item myself, organic where possible and without added sugar, and you pay exactly what it costs, receipts included.
Why does a prep day cost what it does?
You’re not paying for a few hours at the stove. You’re paying for a personal menu, sourcing, cooking, packing and cleanup, six to seven hours of work by someone who has written five cookbooks on exactly this. And you get back hours of your own week.
Do I need to be home?
No. Many clients leave access with their doorman or a key. We’ll agree on what works for you in our consultation.
What do I need in my kitchen?
A stove, an oven and some space in your fridge. I bring my own knives and tools.
How long does the food keep?
Every jar is labeled with the date and reheating tips. Most dishes stay fresh in the fridge for several days, and many can go straight into the freezer.
How do I pay?
You receive an invoice after each prep day, payable by bank transfer, Zelle or card. Monthly plans are billed at the start of the month.
Should I tip?
Never expected, always appreciated.
